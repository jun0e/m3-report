Win rate per level

Bar height = % of attempts that ended in a win. Color band: ≥ 65% PASS 50–65% WARN < 50% KILL. Bosses pinned at the right end.

Median attempts per user (retry burden)

For each level, the median number of attempts a user made before resolving the level (passing OR abandoning). Higher = more retries. King's industry rule of thumb: median ≤ 2 is easy, 3–5 is moderate, > 5 starts to churn players. Most bars sit at 1.0 because the typical user only attempts each level once — the few heavy retriers don't move the median. See the mean table below for the long-tail-sensitive view.

Mean attempts per user — sortable table

Mean = total attempts ÷ unique users. Unlike median, this is sensitive to the long retry tail — a single user who retried a level 10× pulls the mean up noticeably. Sorted by mean DESC (most grindy at top). Click any header to re-sort.
